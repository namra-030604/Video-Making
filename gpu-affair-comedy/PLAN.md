# GPU Affair Comedy — upgraded brief

## Request as a quality bar
A **30-second original comedy**, drawn in code, about a **fictional** “chip boss” having an affair — not with a person, with a **graphics card**. Every shot should read as Saturday-morning slapstick: big poses, readable faces, moving backgrounds, karaoke captions, and a punchline. This is satire. No real names, logos, or likenesses.

## Format
- Length: ~30 s (fit by editing words, Kokoro speed 1.0)
- Aspect: 9:16 (720×1280, 30 fps) — short comedy reel
- Audience: tech-comedy / Shorts
- Style: original cartoon, lime-on-navy, leather-jacket CEO caricature invented for this pack
- Sources: none (fiction). Uncertain claims stay on-screen as FICTION.

## Element sweep
- Backgrounds: 4–6 planes (sky/city, architecture, props, foreground), grain + neon glow
- Characters: Chip (leather jacket), GPU (green date), CPU (dumped beige chip) — distinct silhouettes, large in frame
- Faces/hands/feet: constructed, planted contact shadows
- Props: roses, champagne, ice cream, cameras, podium, coolant glass
- Particles: dust, camera flashes, hearts, sparks
- Motion: hold-and-snap poses; camera ease per shot; nothing static > 0.75 s
- Secondary action: waiter, frog photographer, curtain GPU wave
- Lip-sync: mouth opens on active caption word
- Voice: Kokoro `af_heart` 1.0
- Sound: pad bed + SFX on keywords
- Subtitles: karaoke, lower safe zone, never over faces
- Hook: first 2 s “Breaking news…”
- Ending: “It was just inference. All fiction.”

## Carry-forward
Karaoke captions, -14 LUFS, stills-before-render, pure `renderFrame(t)`, free local voice, restart-safe parts.
